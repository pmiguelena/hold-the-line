The game · El juego

Four crises

The 1973 oil shock, the 2008 crash, the 2020 pandemic, and a calm economy that won't stay calm — each starting where public debt really stood. Cuatro crisis, de 1973 a hoy.

A whole central bank

Rates, forward guidance, asset purchases and their unwinding, a board that votes, a budget for your staff, mortgage rules, reserves, and a Treasury whose debt you cannot ignore. Todo un banco central.

Career mode

Four eras, one Bank. Your credibility and your institution carry forward, if the President reappoints you. Modo carrera.

For teachers · Para docentes

One link for the class

Pick a scenario, shocks and settings; share one link. Every student plays the same economy. Un enlace para toda la clase.

Results you can trust

Students hand in a short result code. The teacher desk replays each game to check it, then gives you a table, a CSV and a printable report per student. Resultados verificables.

Your own scenarios

Write the shocks and the headlines: a commodity boom, a banking panic, a drought. Crea tus propios escenarios.

Open the teacher desk · Abrir el escritorio docente →

What it teaches: lags, expectations and credibility, the Taylor rule, the lower bound, forward guidance, central bank independence, financial stability, open-economy trade-offs. After each term, a debrief compares every decision with the rule and with what the real Fed did. Qué enseña: rezagos, expectativas, credibilidad, regla de Taylor, independencia del banco central.

Based on a New Keynesian model first built in R Shiny (2024). Free to use in class. · Source on GitHub
